# Managing your organisation's users
audience: all

## Who manages customer users

Each customer organisation manages its own portal users. One or more people in your organisation are portal administrators. They decide who can use the portal and with which role. Getronics sets up your first administrator when your portal is opened, and after that your administrators look after your user list themselves.

If you are a portal administrator, you see a Users module in the navigation. Other users do not.

If you need access, or a different role, ask your organisation's portal administrator. Getronics staff cannot add customer users for you.

## The Users screen

The Users screen, titled "Who in your organisation can use the portal, and with which role", lists your organisation's users. Each row shows:

- Name.
- Role.
- Administrator: Yes if the user can manage your organisation's users.
- Subscriptions: All, or Some if the user can see only some of your cloud subscriptions.
- Access: Active, or the date their access was removed.
- Object id: their unique identifier in your organisation's directory.
- Actions: Change, Remove or Restore.

Filter the list by Role.

## Adding a user

Choose "Add a user". Enter:

- Object id: the user's unique identifier in your organisation's directory. If the person has already tried to sign in, the portal showed them their object id on the "no access yet" page, so they can send it to you.
- Name.
- Role: choose from the roles agreed between your organisation and Getronics. Each role is listed with a short description.
- "Administers your organisation's users": tick this to make them a portal administrator too.

Choose "Add user". The user can sign in straight away, using their own organisation account.

## Changing or removing a user

To change a user's role or administrator setting, choose Change on their row, make the change and Save. The object id cannot be changed; to give access to a different person, add them as a new user.

To remove a user's access, choose Remove and confirm. They are signed out on their next request. Their row stays in the list with the date their access was removed, so you have a record.

You cannot remove your own access; ask another administrator if that is needed.

To give a removed user access again, choose Restore, check their role and administrator setting, and save.

Only the roles agreed with your organisation are available. If you need a role that is not in the list, contact your Getronics service manager.
