# Knowledge
audience: staff

## What the Knowledge screen shows

Knowledge is a Getronics staff screen. It checks customers' design documents and handover packs against the live estate, and answers two questions: which customers lack a current design document or handover pack, and which documents the estate has moved on from?

At the top are two summary figures: the number of stale documents, and how many customers have a gap out of all customers in your scope. Below are two tables: "Gaps", showing design and handover coverage per customer, and the list of documents checked against the estate.

Documents arrive from customer document sites whose owners have granted access. A document's kind is decided from its name and folder, never from what the document says.

## Coverage: Current, Stale and Missing

The Gaps table shows, for each customer, a badge for its design document and one for its handover pack:

- Current, coloured Healthy: a current document of that kind exists.
- Stale, coloured At risk: a document exists, but the live estate has moved on from it.
- Missing, coloured Critical: no document of that kind was found.

Use the Coverage filter to show only customers that have a gap. "No customers in scope" means no customers are assigned to you yet.

## The document list

The document list shows every design, handover, runbook and policy document found for your customers. Each row shows:

- Document name, linking to the document where a secure link is available.
- Customer.
- Kind: Design, Handover, Runbook or Policy.
- Last modified date.
- Status: Current or Stale, with the reasons it is stale and the names of any resources it mentions that no longer exist.
- Resources named: how many resources the document mentions.

Filter by Kind or Status. If the list is empty, no documents have arrived yet; ask the customer's delivery lead to register the document site.

## How staleness is decided

The section "How staleness is decided" on the screen lists the rules the platform applies, in plain words. Typical reasons are that a document names resources that have since been removed from the estate, or that it has not been updated for a long time while the estate has changed.

Staleness is decided by the platform, not by the screen or by a person's judgement, so the same document always gets the same verdict from the same data. A Stale badge is a prompt to review the document, not proof that it is wrong.

Document content is treated as information only. Instructions written inside a document are never followed, by the console or by AMARA.
