# Service performance
audience: all

## What the Performance screen shows

The Performance screen shows how well your service has run over the last six months, by calendar month. Months follow UTC.

At the top are two cards about right now: Open incidents and SLA breached, open. Each carries a health badge and links to the Work queue filtered to the tickets behind it.

Below is a monthly table with these columns:

- Month.
- Incidents opened in that month.
- Incidents resolved in that month.
- Resolved within SLA, as a percentage.
- Changes in that month.
- Service requests in that month.

The newest month is at the top. It is the landing screen for the VP or director role.

## Resolved within SLA

"Resolved within SLA" is the SLA attainment for the month: of the incidents resolved in that month, the percentage that were resolved inside their service level target. It is shown to one decimal place, for example 96.4%.

A higher figure is better. 100% means every incident resolved that month met its target.

A dash means there is nothing to measure, for example because no incidents with a service level were resolved that month. A dash is not 0%.

Attainment is based on when incidents were resolved, so an incident opened in one month and resolved in the next counts towards the later month.

## Reading the monthly volumes

The volume columns help you see trends. A rise in Incidents opened with no matching rise in Incidents resolved suggests a growing backlog. A month with many Changes may explain a month with more incidents.

The current month is included and still filling up, so its figures will grow until the month ends. Compare complete months with each other for a fair picture.

"No tickets in the last six months" means no tickets have synced for your organisation yet.

## Open incidents and SLA breached cards

The Open incidents card counts incidents that are open now. Its badge is Healthy below 5, Watch from 5, At risk from 20 and Critical from 50.

The SLA breached, open card counts open tickets that have already missed their target. Its badge is Healthy at 0, Watch from 1, At risk from 3 and Critical from 10.

Choose "View the evidence" on either card to open the Work queue with the matching tickets.
