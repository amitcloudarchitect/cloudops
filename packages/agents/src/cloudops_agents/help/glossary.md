# Glossary
audience: all

## Terms A to C

Amortized cost: cost with up-front purchases, such as reservations, spread across the period they cover, rather than counted on the day they were bought.

AMARA: the AI assistant in the console. It answers questions about the platform and the data you can see. It is read-only and never changes anything.

Approver: a customer role for people who approve changes. See also Approvals.

Approvals: the screen listing open changes that affect your services. It is read-only; approvals are given in the service desk.

As of: the time the data behind a figure was current, shown in UTC.

Band: see Health band.

Breached: a ticket that has missed its SLA target time.

Change request: a planned change to a service. Its individual pieces of work are change tasks.

Change task: one piece of work carried out as part of a change request.

Configuration item (CI): a resource or service the service desk tracks, such as a server or a database, that a ticket is about.

Contract currency: the currency of a customer's contract, used for report packs and margin.

Customer viewer: a general read-only customer role.

## Terms D to L

Dead letter: a record a connector could not process, kept for a person to look at. Getronics staff only.

Draft (report): a report pack still being prepared, or an AI-written suggestion for a summary. AI drafts are never released without a person's text.

Evidence: the list behind a figure. "View the evidence" on a card opens it.

Export CSV: downloads the rows a table is showing as a spreadsheet file.

Facet: a drop-down filter above a table, such as Kind or Severity.

Finding: a vulnerability reported by the scanner on a resource.

FX rate: a foreign exchange rate used to convert between currencies. The rate in force on the transaction date is used.

Health band: the console's single scale for how worrying a value is: Healthy, Watch, At risk, Critical, or Unknown.

Incident: a ticket raised because something is broken or degraded and needs restoring.

Landing screen: the screen your role opens on after you sign in.

## Terms M to P

Made SLA: a resolved ticket that met its SLA target.

Measured: a figure counted or read directly from a source.

Modelled: a figure calculated from a model or assumptions rather than observed. Marked "Modelled" in the console.

Not captured: a figure that cannot be produced yet because an input is missing. The reason is shown. It is never shown as zero.

Not yet priced: consumption that has been recorded but whose price has not been captured. Your Getronics service manager can confirm the rate.

Portal administrator: a customer user who manages their organisation's portal users on the Users screen.

Preset view: a ready-made combination of filters and sort for a table, chosen for your role.

Priority: the urgency given to a ticket in the service desk, from 1 (highest) to 5.

Project task: a ticket that belongs to a project rather than day-to-day operations.

Published: a report pack released to the customer.

## Terms Q to S

QID: the vulnerability scanner's identifier for a type of vulnerability.

Quarantine: where records are held when their customer name or resource could not be matched. They are never guessed or dropped. Getronics staff only.

Remediated: a vulnerability finding that a later scan no longer reports.

Request (service request): a ticket asking for something, such as access or a new resource, rather than reporting a fault.

Resource: an item in your cloud subscriptions, such as a virtual machine, database or storage account.

Saved view: a combination of filters and sort you saved on a table. Kept in your browser.

Severity: how serious a vulnerability is, graded 1 (Minimal) to 5 (Urgent).

SLA: service level agreement, the target time for handling a ticket.

SLA attainment: the percentage of incidents resolved in a month that made SLA. Shown as "Resolved within SLA".

Source: the system a figure came from, such as ServiceNow, Qualys or Cloud billing.

Superseded: a report pack replaced by a newer version for the same month.

## Terms T to Z

Tag: a label set on a cloud resource, such as owner, environment or cost centre. Used to allocate cost and identify owners.

Ticket: any work item from the service desk: an incident, change task, request or project task.

Triage: the first assessment of a new incident, made automatically and reviewed by Getronics staff. It never changes anything in your environment.

Unallocated: cost that could not be assigned to an application or cost centre, usually because of missing tags.

Unblended cost: the cost charged on the day, without spreading up-front purchases.

Unknown: the health band shown when there is not enough data to judge.

Unverified: cost that has not yet been confirmed against the provider's own total. Getronics staff only.

UTC: Coordinated Universal Time. All times and months in the console use UTC.

Within: a ticket that has not missed its SLA target.

Work queue: the screen listing your tickets from the service desk.
