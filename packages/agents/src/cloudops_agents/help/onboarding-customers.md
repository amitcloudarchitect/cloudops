# Onboarding customers to the portal
audience: staff

## What Portal onboarding is

Portal onboarding is a Getronics staff screen, used by platform administrators to open the customer portal to a customer. A customer must already exist in the platform, created through Customer mapping, before it appears here.

The table lists each customer with its Portal status, its identity provider, its email domains, how many roles have been agreed, and the date it went live. Filter by Portal status. "No customers in your scope" means none are assigned to you, or none have been created yet.

Onboarding has four steps, which must be done in order: federation, the agreed role set, the first customer administrator, and go-live. Each step shows "To do" or the date it was completed.

## Portal statuses

A customer's portal is in one of four states:

- Not started: no onboarding step has been recorded.
- Setting up: onboarding is in progress but the portal is not open.
- Live: the customer's users can sign in.
- Suspended: the portal has been closed again; users cannot use it.

A customer whose portal is not Live cannot use the portal, even if users have been added.

## The four onboarding steps

1. Federation. Record how the customer's users sign in: their identity provider and the email domains they use. The customer's users then sign in with their own organisation accounts.

2. Role set agreed with the customer. Choose which of the customer roles the customer may use, and record where the agreement is kept. The customer's administrator can only assign roles from this set.

3. First customer administrator. Assign the first person at the customer who will manage their own users. Give their object id, name and role.

4. Go live. Open the portal. The same step lets you suspend a live portal if needed.

After go-live, Getronics does not manage the customer's user list. The customer's own administrators add and remove their users from the Users screen.
