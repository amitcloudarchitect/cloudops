# Approvals
audience: all

## What the Approvals screen shows

The Approvals screen lists open changes that affect your services, oldest first. It is the landing screen for the Approver role, and it helps you see at a glance what is waiting for a decision.

Each row shows:

- Change task, the task number. If your role can see tickets, it links to the ticket page.
- Change request, the change it belongs to.
- Summary.
- Priority, as a badge.
- Configuration item, the resource or service it affects.
- State, as recorded in the service desk.
- Opened, the date it was raised.

Getronics staff who look after several customers also see a Customer column. The count above the table shows its source and "as of" time.

## Approving or rejecting a change

The Approvals screen is read-only for now. A note at the top says so. You cannot approve or reject a change from the console.

To approve or reject, use the service desk (ServiceNow) as you do today. The console picks up your decision at the next sync, usually within a few minutes, and the change leaves this list once it is no longer waiting.

AMARA cannot approve or reject changes either. It can list what is waiting, explain what a change affects, or tell you how long it has been open.

## When the list is empty

"Nothing awaiting approval" means there are no open changes affecting your services right now. New changes appear here as they are raised in the service desk and synced.

If you expected a change to be listed and it is not, check the "as of" time above the table, and confirm in the service desk that the change is open and assigned to your organisation.
