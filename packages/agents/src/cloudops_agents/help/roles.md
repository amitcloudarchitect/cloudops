# Roles
audience: all

## How roles work

Your role decides which screens you see and where you land when you sign in. The navigation shows only the modules your role can use. AMARA uses the same role, so it can answer only about data your role can see.

Customer users are given a role by their organisation's portal administrator, chosen from the roles agreed between the organisation and Getronics. The default customer roles are described below; your organisation may use some or all of them. A customer role never includes Getronics' commercial figures or platform administration.

Anyone can also be made a portal administrator for their organisation, in addition to their role. Administrators see the Users screen and manage their organisation's users.

## CXO and VP or director

The CXO role is for executives who want the headline picture. It lands on the Customers screen, which shows service health, SLA, cloud consumption and security posture at a glance. It can see tickets, resources, cost (your cloud consumption at the price you are charged) and vulnerabilities, so it can also open the Work queue, Performance, Security posture and Resources screens.

The VP or director role is for service owners who track performance over time. It lands on the Performance screen, which shows SLA attainment and ticket volumes by month for the last six months. It can see tickets, resources and cost. It does not include vulnerability findings, so Security posture is not in its navigation.

## Architect and Customer engineer

The Architect role is for people responsible for the design of the cloud estate. It lands on the Resources screen, where each resource shows its details, tags, incidents and recent changes. It can see resources, cost and knowledge. It does not include the ticket list or vulnerability findings.

The Customer engineer role is for hands-on technical staff who follow tickets day to day. It lands on the Work queue, with preset views for incidents and for SLA-breached tickets. It can see tickets, resources and knowledge. It does not include cost or vulnerability findings.

## Security officer and Approver

The Security officer role is for people responsible for security risk. It lands on the Security posture screen, which shows open vulnerabilities by severity and by how long they have been open. It can see vulnerabilities and resources. It does not include tickets or cost.

The Approver role is for people who approve changes on behalf of the organisation. It lands on the Approvals screen, which lists open changes that affect your services. That screen is read-only: approving and rejecting still happen in the service desk. The Approver role can see approvals and resources.

## Customer viewer

The Customer viewer role is a general read-only role. It lands on the Customers screen and gives a broad view of your operations and your cloud consumption, without any administration.

If you are unsure which role you have, look at the navigation: it lists the screens your role includes. If a screen a colleague mentions is missing, your role does not include it. Your organisation's portal administrator can tell you your role and change it if needed. AMARA can also explain what a role includes, but it cannot change your role.

## Getronics staff roles

Getronics staff have roles that match their job, such as engineers, delivery leads, finance and platform administrators. Each staff member sees only the customers assigned to them, and only the screens their role includes.

Some screens are for Getronics staff only, such as FinOps, Margin, Pricing, Knowledge, Assurance, Connectors, Customer mapping and Portal onboarding. Commercial figures are visible only to authorised Getronics staff. Customers never see these screens.
