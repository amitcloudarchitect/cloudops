# Reports
audience: all

## What monthly report packs are

A report pack is a monthly report for one customer, generated from the platform's live data. It brings together the month's figures, such as service performance, security and cloud cost, in sections, each with a short written summary.

Every pack is in the customer's contract currency, and states the time its data was taken from ("data as of"). A pack is a snapshot: later changes to the data do not alter it. To reflect new data, a new version of the pack is generated.

Getronics prepares and reviews each pack before it reaches the customer. Nothing is published without a person having written or accepted the summary text.

## The Reports list

The Reports screen lists packs. Each row shows the pack (customer, month and version), its Status, its Contract currency, the date it was Generated and the date it was Published. Choose a pack to open it.

Filter by Status or Month. The list opens with the most recently generated first.

If you are a customer user, you see only your organisation's published packs, and only if your role includes reports. Reports are not part of the default customer roles; if you would like access, ask your organisation's portal administrator or your Getronics service manager.

## Pack statuses

A pack moves through these statuses:

- Draft: being prepared. Figures are generated; summaries are being written and reviewed.
- Approved, not published: checked and approved, but not yet released to the customer.
- Published: released to the customer.
- Superseded: replaced by a newer version for the same month.

Customers see Published packs only. A pack's status is shown as a badge on the list and at the top of the pack page. When a newer version of a month is published, the older one becomes Superseded; use the newest version.

## Reading a pack

A pack's page is titled with the customer and month. Under the title you see the version, the contract currency, and when the data was taken.

Each section lists its figures, rounded as they appear in the printed report: money in the contract currency, percentages to two decimal places, durations in days or hours. If a section could not be filled, it says "Not captured" with the reason, rather than showing zeros.

Each section has a written summary. Choose "Download PDF" to save the pack as a PDF. The PDF contains only text written or accepted by a person.

## Preparing and releasing a pack (Getronics staff)

Staff whose role allows it can generate a pack: choose the customer and month, and the pack opens as a Draft. A pack cannot be generated for a month that has not started, or for a month with no statement of work in force, because the contract currency is then unknown.

For each summary section, an AI draft may be offered. A draft is never printed and never released. Write the narrative, or choose "Start from the draft" and edit it, then save. If a draft was declined, for example because it used a number not in the data, the reason is shown; you can request a new draft.

Approval waits until every section has a person's text. Approve, then Publish when the pack should reach the customer. Only a Draft can be edited; to change a published pack, generate a new version.

## Portfolio view (Getronics staff)

Below the pack list, Getronics staff see a Portfolio table for a chosen month and reporting currency (EUR, GBP or USD). It lists each customer in scope with cloud cost converted into that currency, incidents opened, and open severity 4 and 5 vulnerabilities, with a total.

Each cost row is converted at its own rate. If some rows have no rate for the chosen currency, the total is marked incomplete and those customers show the reason instead of a figure.
